# Azure Cost Management

## 1. What Affects Azure Costs?

Azure pricing depends on several factors:

- **Resource Type:** VMs, storage, databases, etc. have different prices.
- **Usage:** How much of a service you consume.
- **Region:** Prices can vary depending on the Azure region.
- **Data Transfer:** Incoming data is generally free, while outgoing data may incur charges.
- **Subscription and Pricing Options:** Discounts and billing arrangements can affect costs.

**Remember:** Billing zones and Availability Zones are different. Billing zones relate to data-transfer pricing, while Availability Zones provide datacenter failure protection.

## 2. Azure Purchasing Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise Agreement (EA)** | Negotiated Azure purchasing arrangement for organizations |
| **Web Direct** | Purchase Azure services directly through Microsoft |
| **Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)** | Purchase and manage Azure services through a Microsoft partner |

## 4. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Calculator

**TCO Calculator** estimates potential savings when moving from on-premises infrastructure to Azure.

It compares costs such as servers, storage, electricity, software, and maintenance.

**Remember: TCO Calculator → Compare on-premises costs with Azure costs.**

## 5. Azure Cost Management

**Azure Cost Management** helps organizations monitor, analyze, and optimize their Azure spending.

It provides:

- **Cost Analysis:** Understand where money is being spent.
- **Budgets:** Set spending targets.
- **Alerts:** Receive notifications when costs approach or exceed budget thresholds.
- **Forecasting:** Estimate future spending.

**Important:** Budget alerts notify you about spending; they don't automatically stop resources.

## 6. Ways to Reduce Azure Costs

| Method | How It Helps |
|---|---|
| **Azure Advisor** | Recommends removing or resizing underused resources |
| **Azure Reservations** | Offers discounts for eligible resources with 1-year or 3-year commitments |
| **Choose Regions Carefully** | Deploy in cost-effective regions when suitable |
| **Resource Tags** | Organize resources and track costs by department or project |
| **Spending Limits** | Restrict spending on eligible credit-based subscriptions |

Spending limits are not available for standard pay-as-you-go subscriptions.

## Key Takeaway

- **Pricing Calculator** → Estimate Azure service costs.

- **Cost Management** → Monitor spending, budgets, and alerts.

- **Tags** → Organize and track resource costs.
